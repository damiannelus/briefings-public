## Macro Spotlight – Deep Dive  
**Strategic Thread: Regulatory and Cybersecurity Challenges in Critical Sectors**  
The EU’s Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR) is imposing significant administrative and financial burdens on micro-entrepreneurs, making it economically unviable for many to operate within the EU. This regulation requires small businesses to register and comply with packaging waste obligations in each EU country where they operate, leading to high costs and bureaucratic hurdles. The article highlights that the current regulatory framework is not aligned with the needs of small-scale businesses, which are crucial for innovation and economic diversity. The EU must address these issues to prevent the loss of a vibrant ecosystem of micro-enterprises and independent creators.  

Simultaneously, over 100 U.S. water systems were targeted in July 2026 cyberattacks, often through internet-exposed programmable logic controllers (PLCs). These attacks, suspected to be linked to Iran, reveal a systemic vulnerability in critical infrastructure, as many systems rely on outdated operational technology (OT) that was not designed for internet connectivity. Experts warn that these attacks may be test runs for larger-scale incidents, especially with the increasing use of AI-generated exploitation scripts. CISA has issued recommendations to improve security, such as keeping PLCs off the public internet and using secure remote access methods. The incident underscores the urgent need for stronger cybersecurity measures in critical infrastructure sectors.  

These two issues—regulatory burdens on small businesses and cybersecurity vulnerabilities in critical infrastructure—highlight the need for a balanced approach that supports innovation while ensuring resilience and security. Policymakers and industry leaders must work together to address these challenges, ensuring that regulatory frameworks are both effective and equitable, and that cybersecurity measures are robust enough to protect against evolving threats.
