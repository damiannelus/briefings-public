## Macro Spotlight – Deep Dive  
**AI in Software Migration: A Game-Changer**  
The integration of AI into software migration processes is revolutionizing how organizations approach large-scale technical transitions. Companies like Asana, Airbnb, and Uber have leveraged AI tools such as OpenAI Codex and LLMs to complete complex migrations in weeks, saving millions in costs. For instance, Asana migrated from Enzyme to React Testing Library in two weeks, while Airbnb completed a 3,500-test migration in six weeks using LLMs. These examples demonstrate that AI is not only reducing the time and cost of migrations but also making previously impractical projects feasible. The use of AI in migration is expected to become more widespread as the technology continues to evolve, with companies increasingly relying on AI to handle long-avoided migration projects. This shift is not only changing the way engineering teams approach infrastructure changes but also redefining the role of AI in software development. As AI models improve and become more accessible, the potential for even more ambitious and complex migrations will only grow, with significant implications for how organizations plan and execute large-scale technical transitions.
